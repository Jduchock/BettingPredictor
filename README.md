# BettingPredictor

Fictitious betting predictor — an ongoing test of AI sports-betting analysis using a $5,000 pretend account. **No real money is wagered.** For entertainment purposes only.

## 📊 Account Dashboard (start here)

**➡️ [Open the Account Dashboard](https://jduchock.github.io/BettingPredictor/account-dashboard.html)**

Live roll-up of the whole experiment: account balance, active card slots, per-card P/L, and the 🎲 longshot tracker. Tap any card row to open its full betting card. The page checks for fresh scores about every 10 minutes during live games and reloads itself automatically.

## Other links

- **[Site hub](https://jduchock.github.io/BettingPredictor/)** — all betting cards (active + archived), PDF versions, and the Excel tracker download
- Each card also has a PDF companion for easy sharing (same filename, `.pdf`)

## How it works

Every test event gets a $1,000 card (max 4 active at once) built from public odds and stats research, always including one mandatory longshot. Results are graded against the odds recorded at card creation, published here via GitHub Pages, and rolled up into the account dashboard and the `betting_tracker.xlsx` spreadsheet.
